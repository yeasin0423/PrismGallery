Gallery is a fast, private gallery for your photos and videos. It works entirely offline: the app has no internet permission, no ads, no tracking and no account.

It is made for custom ROMs that ship without a usable gallery, for phones whose only gallery is a cloud photo app, and for anyone who wants a smooth, good-looking gallery.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>A Samsung One UI-inspired design with its own layout and animations</li>
<li>Photos, videos and GIFs from internal storage and the SD card</li>
<li>Albums: create, rename, hide, sub-albums, move media between albums, move whole albums between internal storage and SD card</li>
<li>Favorites and trash</li>
<li>Deep zoom, readable even on huge photos and very long scroll screenshots</li>
<li>Editor: crop, rotate and mirror photos and videos (saved as a copy)</li>
<li>Storage overview for internal storage and SD card</li>
<li>Open photos and videos from other apps ("Open with")</li>
<li>English</li>
<li>Adjustable graphics quality for older or low-memory devices</li>
</ul>

Requires Android 10 or newer.

Gallery is an independent project and is not affiliated with Samsung. The name only describes the visual style the design is inspired by.
